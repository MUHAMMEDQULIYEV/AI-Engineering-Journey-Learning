# 🌧️ Bad days: what to do when you feel tired, lost or unmotivated

## Rule 1 (no thinking needed)
> **Open the file. Run 1 cell. Set a 5-minute timer.**
> After 5 minutes you are allowed to stop.

Most of the time you won't stop. **Motivation comes after you start, not before.**

If it still feels bad after 5 minutes, tell your AI tutor:
```
I feel <X>. Today's task is <Y>. Give me the smallest version of it.
```

---

## Which feeling is it?
Read this table on a calm day (at your weekly review), not in the bad moment.

| Feeling | What it usually is | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Sleepy *only* when study starts, but fine for videos and games | Starting friction (your brain avoids the hard first step) | Rule 1. The first step must be tiny: 1 cell, 1 bug, 1 predict. |
| "I don't get it, I'm stupid" | The step is too big | Make the step smaller. Ask the AI for a smaller step + one predict question. Don't escape to a new video or course. |
| "I already know this", bored | Too easy, or passive watching | Prove it: explain it from memory, or code it with no video. If it's really easy, skip ahead. |
| Really tired (bad sleep, long day) | Your body | **Minimum day:** 15 minutes + 1 commit (notes count). End session is 2 lines: NEXT + commit. Then rest without guilt. |
| Sad or stressed (exams, family, relationship, money) | Life | Take a rest day. Write one line about it in your weekly check-in. Tomorrow is a minimum day. |
| Guilty when resting, "I should do more" | Overwork | Rest is part of the plan. It's a 10-month marathon (Oct → Jul). Put extra energy into a walk or the `IDEAS.md` list, not into tomorrow's task. |
| Low for many days in a row: no energy, no joy, sleep or eating changed | Maybe more than motivation | Talk to a friend or family member and to your university's counseling service or a doctor. Health first; the plan waits. |

---

## Always true
- **The minimum day counts.** 15 minutes + 1 commit keeps the chain alive. A commit of only notes counts.
- **Never miss twice.** One missed study day is normal. Two *planned* study days in a row is the old pattern (optional days and planned rest days don't count).
- **No restart after a bad week.** Don't make a new plan. Continue from your `NEXT.md`.
- **Look at what you already did:** your commit history. It is your list of wins.
- **You're not lazy.** If you can go to the gym or show up to class, you can be consistent. The problem is usually *not knowing the next step*, and the plan + NEXT line solve that.
