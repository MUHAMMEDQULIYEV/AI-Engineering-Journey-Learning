Rules that keep it going

  • One course + one project open at a time.
  • Every session ends with a commit; in exam weeks, notes count.
  • Number targets are reported, timeboxed ≤2 days, then shipped.
  • One REVIEW session a week is AI-off.
  • Never miss two planned study days in a row.
  • From Jan: a weekly 45-min slot for DSA/SQL or a blank-file rebuild.
