1 · Raw text: what the computer actually sees

To the computer, the corpus is one long string of characters. Spaces and line breaks are characters too. Below, · is a space and ↵ is a newline.

2 · Sentences (sent_tokenize)

First cut: split the corpus into sentences. It splits after . ! ? when whitespace follows. Commas and line breaks do not end a sentence.

5 · Numbers: tokens become IDs

Models can't read strings. Each vocab entry gets an integer ID (here: sorted order), and the text becomes a list of IDs. This list is what goes into the model. It's like class labels in FashionMNIST: just indices.
